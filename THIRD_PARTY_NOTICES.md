# Third-Party Data

## Natural Earth 1:50m Land

- Source: [Natural Earth Vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector)
- File: `ne_50m_land.geojson`
- Contents: generalized global land polygons, including islands and archipelagos
- License: Public Domain
- Local copy: `app/src/main/assets/natural_earth_land.geojson`

The coastline data is geographic reference data. The application projects these coordinates into its selected experimental flat-map projection; that visualization does not imply that the projection is a true representation of Earth's physical shape.
